What I automate most often

Making two services talk

Two tools exchange data on their own as soon as something happens: an email arrives, a contact changes, a file is dropped.

Enriching and cleaning a CRM / SRM

From incoming emails, scraping or any external data. Every email is qualified by a service like ZeroBounce, then tagged and cleaned in the database: quality data that is easy to find.

Processing images and files

Reading images with OCR (business cards, scans) and converting files: a dropped PDF becomes a Word document.

Answering customer support

Customer service replies are automated by an AI agent, with handover to a person for complex cases.

Data, security & ownership

Your accounts, your workflows

Automations run in your own n8n or Power Automate environment. You own the workflows, credentials and documentation, with no lock-in.

Minimal access

I ask only for the permissions a workflow needs, keep credentials in the tool's secure vault and work within your IT policies.

Compliant data sourcing

I use professional contact data from legitimate sources, document the source and legal basis with you, and build opt-out and suppression lists into every outreach workflow.

NDA before access

I sign an NDA before touching your data, and your data stays in your own tools instead of being copied elsewhere.

Toolbox

Automation
  • n8nA workflow builder that connects your apps to each other and runs tasks for you, without anyone clicking or copy-pasting.
  • Power AutomateMicrosoft's automation tool. When something happens in Outlook, Teams or SharePoint, like a new email or a dropped file, it starts the next steps for you.
  • PythonA programming language I use for custom jobs that ready-made tools cannot do, such as cleaning large files or generating documents automatically.
  • AI toolsAI assistants and agents that read, sort, summarize and answer text for you, such as support requests or incoming emails.
Data collection
  • Web scrapingAutomatically gathers public information from web pages, such as company details, prices or job offers, and puts it in a clean table.
  • Web crawlingAutomatically explores a website, page after page, to discover and list everything worth collecting, so nothing is missed.
  • ParsingReads raw emails, text or files and pulls out only the useful fields (name, email, company, amount) in a structured format.
  • OCRReads the text in images and scans, such as business cards or paper documents, and turns it into editable data.
Data & CRM
  • ZohoA CRM: the company's address book, where customers, prospects and suppliers are stored and followed. I keep it clean and up to date automatically.
  • BrevoAn emailing platform to send campaigns to many contacts and track the results. I connect it to your CRM so lists and results stay in sync.
  • ZeroBounceAn email verification service that checks an address is valid before you send, to avoid bounces and protect your sender reputation.
  • ExcelSpreadsheets for lists, reports and imports. I clean, reformat and generate Excel files automatically so nobody fixes them by hand.
  • SharePointMicrosoft's shared file space. A file dropped in a folder can trigger an automation, like importing contacts and emailing a summary.

Questions

Do you work with my stack?

I have shipped on Zoho, Brevo, ZeroBounce, Outlook, SharePoint and Excel. For HubSpot, Slack, Notion, Airtable or others, n8n and REST APIs connect to most tools. If I have not used a tool before, I tell you and run a short test before we commit.

How do you measure results?

I report what the automation controls: volume processed, bounce rate, hours saved, workflows running. Reply and conversion rates depend on your offer, so we track them together.

What happens if something breaks?

Workflows are monitored so I hear about failures before your team does. On a care plan I fix and adapt them; otherwise you get the documentation to run them yourselves.

Which languages and time zones?

English and French, working remotely from France (CET).
